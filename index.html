01 — HERO CASE STUDY 2026 Digital Solution & Business Process Analysis PT PLN Indonesia Power UBP Semarang

SIPRAKER — Internship Attendance System

Business Process Analysis · Requirements · System Implementation · Testing

Role: Business Process & System Analysis | Developer

SIPRAKER is an internal internship attendance system developed for PT PLN Indonesia Power UBP Semarang.

The project started from an existing attendance process. I discussed the workflow with Humas administrators to understand how the process worked and what users needed, mapped the existing process using BPMN, translated the findings into system requirements and functions, and then developed and tested the system.

1 The Problem

The existing attendance workflow relied on physical sign-in sheets and manual leave coordination. This caused operational friction for Humas administrators who needed to manually verify attendance, reconcile leave records at the end of each internship period, and validate official completion certificates without a centralized digital log.

2 Understanding the Existing Process (AS-IS)

To clarify where operational bottlenecks and data gaps occurred, I mapped the complete AS-IS attendance workflow using BPMN 2.0 in Bizagi Modeler. The diagram helped identify manual touchpoints, delayed status updates, and the absence of real-time validation between participants and administrators.

AS-IS Attendance Workflow

Mapping of the existing manual attendance process used to understand activities, roles, and points that could be supported by the digital system.

3 User & Process Needs

👤
Internship Participants

Needed a straightforward, mobile-friendly way to check in daily, submit leave requests with notes, and view their attendance history.

🏢
Humas Administrators

Needed real-time daily oversight, automated monthly attendance summaries, easy leave review, and instant certificate authenticity verification.

4 From Process to Requirements

Findings from the AS-IS process analysis and administrator discussions were directly translated into actionable system requirements and functions:

Existing Activity / Gap System Requirement Implemented System Function
Manual paper sign-in roster Digital timestamped check-in with location/status tracking Daily Attendance Recording Module
Informal manual leave requests Structured leave submission with status tracking & admin approval Leave Submission & Approval Flow
Manual recap calculation for reporting Centralized dashboard with automated recap filtering Admin Oversight & Monthly Recap Generation
Unverified physical certificate records Public QR-code verification for completed internship certificates Public QR Verification Endpoint

5 Proposed Process (TO-BE)

I modeled the TO-BE process flow illustrating how SIPRAKER integrates into the daily routine. The digital system eliminates manual paper logs, automates validation rules, and provides immediate visibility for Humas administrators.

TO-BE Process Workflow

Improved workflow showing automated attendance recording, digital leave reviews, and integrated reporting.

6 System Implementation & Scope

System Scope
✓ Attendance recording ✓ Leave submission ✓ Admin dashboard ✓ Attendance recap ✓ Public QR verification
Admin Dashboard Implementation

Provides Humas administrators with high-level attendance metrics, participant status logs, and quick actions for review.

7 Functional Testing

Functional testing was conducted across all core modules. The implemented features—including attendance recording, leave submission validation, recap generation, and QR verification—were verified against the expected workflows and defined requirements.

02 2026 IT Business Analysis & Product Requirements 🏆 3rd Place – Rising Innovator

SpecFlow AI — AI-Assisted Requirements & Impact Analysis

IT Business Analysis · Product Requirements · Solution Design

Role: IT Business Analyst | Refactory Hackathon x Telkom University

SpecFlow AI was a hackathon product concept designed to help teams transform natural-language business requirements into structured specifications and identify potential technical impacts when requirements change.

Our team received 3rd Place – Rising Innovator. As the IT Business Analyst, I led the problem analysis, user needs formulation, functional requirements breakdown, PRD documentation, and C4 context diagram.

1 The Problem & User Need

In fast-paced software projects, spontaneous requirement changes frequently occur. Without automated impact tracing, engineering and product teams spend significant manual effort assessing which architectural modules, endpoints, and databases are affected, increasing the risk of scope creep and release delays.

Core User Story
“As a Project Manager, I want to instantly generate an automated technical impact analysis when business requirements unexpectedly change, so that the engineering team can avoid manually tracing architectural impacts and reduce the risk of project delays.”

2 Product Scope & PRD Deliverable

I defined the product boundaries and structured the functional requirements inside the Product Requirements Document (PRD), focusing on requirement parsing, difference detection, and dependency graph output.

PRD ARTIFACT HIGHLIGHT
SpecFlow AI — Product Requirements Document Structure
  • FR-01: Requirement Parsing: Accepts natural language feature requests and extracts structured entities (actors, actions, data entities).
  • FR-02: Impact Detection: Analyzes modified user stories against connected repository endpoints and schema files.
  • FR-03: Change Summary Report: Generates automated visual change reports for project managers and technical leads.

3 Solution Concept (C4 Context Model)

To clearly communicate how SpecFlow AI interacts with users and external software systems, I structured a C4 Model Level 1 Context Diagram. It clarifies boundaries between Project Managers, the SpecFlow AI analysis engine, LLM services, and Git repositories.

03 2025 Process Improvement & Digital Automation Copyright Protected (HKI)

Process Automation — From Manual Workflow to Digital Process

Process Improvement · Workflow Analysis · Digital Automation

Role: Process Improvement & Automation Contributor

Reviewed an existing administrative workflow, identified manual activities and opportunities for improvement, and supported the development of a Microsoft Power Automate solution.

1 Existing Process & Improvement Opportunity

The original administrative process required staff to manually check inbound submissions, manually copy details across tracking spreadsheets, and send individual notification emails to stakeholders. This manual routine introduced data entry delays, occasional duplicate records, and lacked real-time status tracking.

Original Manual Workflow
  • Manual inbound submission checking
  • Manual copy-pasting of form records
  • Individual email drafting for status updates
  • Fragmented audit trails
Automated Digital Workflow
  • Automated trigger on new submission
  • Instant data parsing and spreadsheet synchronization
  • Triggered notifications and approval requests
  • Consistent, real-time audit logging

2 Automated Workflow Implementation & Outcome

Using Microsoft Power Automate, I structured flow triggers, validation branches, and email dispatch routines to ensure reliable automated execution. The solution eliminated manual repetitive steps and standardized operational data records.

My Contribution

  • Identified manual activities and improvement opportunities
  • Analyzed workflow requirements for automation
  • Supported development using Microsoft Power Automate
  • Validated the automated workflow
  • Documented the improved process

Project Outcome & Recognition

The automated workflow successfully streamlined administrative overhead, reduced data entry errors, and established consistent notification timelines. The resulting automated workflow system received official intellectual property (HKI) copyright protection.
