BACKGROUND & PERSPECTIVE

A little about how I work

I enjoy working in the space between business and technology. My focus is understanding what users and organizations actually need, structuring those needs into clear product specifications, and working alongside technical teams to deliver practical solutions.

When approaching a problem, I like to understand:

  • 01
    What problem users actually face — unpacking friction points and unstated needs rather than accepting surface-level requests.
  • 02
    Why a process works the way it does — analyzing end-to-end workflows, handoffs, and organizational constraints.
  • 03
    Where friction and inefficiencies occur — tracing manual bottlenecks, redundant steps, and data disconnects.
  • 04
    What requirements are actually needed — defining crisp functional scopes, user stories, and acceptance criteria.
  • 05
    How technology can improve the experience — leveraging system architecture, APIs, automation, or AI where it genuinely adds value.
  • 06
    How data can help evaluate the result — tracking operational metrics, event logs, and user feedback to guide iteration.

My technical background in Informatics gives me enough architectural and engineering depth to communicate fluently with developers, understand database constraints, and model system context—while keeping the primary focus on solving user and business problems.

Process Automation

From Manual Workflow to Automated Digital Execution

Role: Process Improvement & Automation Contributor

The Problem & Opportunity

The existing administrative workflow required staff to manually check inbound submissions, manually copy-paste form records across spreadsheets, and draft individual email notifications to stakeholders. This manual routine introduced processing delays and lacked standardized status tracking.

Manual Workflow
  • Manual checking of inbound submissions
  • Repetitive manual copy-pasting into spreadsheets
  • Individual email drafting for status updates
  • Fragmented audit trails and verification
Automated Digital Workflow
  • Automated cloud trigger on new form submission
  • Instant data parsing and spreadsheet record synchronization
  • Triggered notifications and approval routing
  • Standardized, real-time audit logging

My Contribution & Validation

  • Reviewed the manual administrative workflow and identified automation opportunities.
  • Structured workflow logic, conditional branches, and automated triggers in Microsoft Power Automate.
  • Validated flow execution, error handling, and notification accuracy.
  • Documented the improved digital process.
